Privacy

DOOR+ is built so that most of what it knows never leaves your iPhone. This page says exactly what goes where. Updated September 30, 2026.

Stays on your iPhone
  • Your location is used on the phone to tell when you're near a door you added. DOOR+ never uploads your location history.
  • Your doors, their marked spots, learned lock signals and the Activity log are stored on the phone only.
  • Doorcodes, the help number, passwords and tokens (Lockout Kit, Panic, GroupMe, email) are kept in the iPhone's Keychain. If you turn it on, the Lockout Kit is copied to your own Apple Watch.
  • Panic recordings are saved to your own Photos library. DOOR+ doesn't upload them anywhere; if iCloud Photos is on, Apple backs them up like any photo.
Sent when you use a feature
  • Weather: the coordinates of a door spot you marked go to Open-Meteo to get the forecast. No account, nothing else sent.
  • Unlocking: DOOR+ asks the lock's own app (for example DOOR) or your HomeKit lock to unlock. It doesn't send your doors anywhere else.
  • Panic alerts: when you trigger panic, the message (your name, unit, building and location) goes to the GroupMe groups and email addresses you chose, through your own accounts.
  • Group chat: reading and replying to your GroupMe groups goes directly between your phone and GroupMe, with your own token.
Community (only if you sign in)
  • If you join your building's community, DOOR+ stores your name, your posts, clubs you join, reports you file and, only if you make your profile public, your unit and bio on the DOOR+ community server (hosted by Supabase in the United States).
  • Only verified residents of your building, admitted with the building's invite code, can see any of it. Your building's admins can hide posts and read reports.
  • Signing in uses Sign in with Apple or an emailed code. DOOR+ doesn't see your Apple password.
  • Sponsored cards are chosen by your building's admins. DOOR+ doesn't track you across apps or sell data, and has no advertising SDKs.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your iPhone. To delete your community account and posts, sign out in Profile and ask your building's admin, or reply through TestFlight feedback, and it will be removed.

Children

DOOR+ is meant for adult residents and isn't directed at children.
